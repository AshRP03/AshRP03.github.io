Texas SCADA Testbed

With the Texas SCADA Testbed, I work on cybersecurity with cyber-physical systems, where SCADA stands for supervisory control and data acquisition. We work on PLCs, using ladder logic, and simulation software to model critical infrastructure environments. Through the SCADA Lab, I have gotten opportunities like networking with cybersecurity individuals, being a VICEROY scholar, and attending a Department of Energy Cybsersecurity and Innovation Conference.

This project was an answer to a problem made by the startup, Marky.
