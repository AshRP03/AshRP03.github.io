Study Abroad

Exploring a Slice of Europe, but
Through My Lens: Cars, Food, and History.

In Spring 2024, I studied abroad at Swansea University in Wales. Over a semester, I explored Europe on weekends—from the city streets of Vienna to the shores of Lake Como—immersing myself in European nature, culture, and automotive. What began as a university experience quickly evolved into a journey of independence, global awareness, and lasting inspiration.

From Wales to Western Europe, this map traces one of the most meaningful chapters of my life—of movement, memory, and meaning.

Art, Culture & Heritage

From the baroque elegance of Vienna, the pastas of Rome, or the waffles of Brussels, these cities showcased tradition, creativity, and history.

Automotive Pilgrimage

This was a dream for any car enthusiast—legendary museums, tracks, and motorsport heritage that brought automotive passion to life.

Nature & Scenic Moments

Europe's landscapes are surreal—whether it’s the calm of Como, the grandeur of the Alps, or the cliffs of the Welsh coast.

Wales & Campus Life

Swansea was my home base, where studying, making friends, and enjoying everyday life in Wales brought a sense of balance to the adventure.

A World-Class Education, Outside the Classroom

Traveling solo taught me more than independence. It taught me how to observe, how to listen, and how to reflect. Whether I was learning 4 different languages on Duolingo while travelling, wearing my Aggie Ring at the Ferrari factory, or navigating train stations with a sprained ankle in Monaco, I was always carrying a piece of home with me. These experiences deepened my love for the world, for culture, and for asking “why is this place the way it is, and how can I learn more?” It’s a chapter I’ll never forget.

The Spirit of Aggieland Abroad

My Aggie Ring came with me on every leg of this journey—a symbol of where I come from, and what I represent. Whether on scenic cliffsides or bustling cities, it was a small reminder of home and the values I carry wherever I go.
