Mentorship Through the Brown Foundation

As a Craig & Galen Brown Foundation Scholar, I’ve had the privilege of mentoring incoming freshmen scholars as they transition into college life. Whether it’s offering advice about coursework, research, or navigating campus, my goal is to help others find confidence, community, and direction early in their college experience.

I've learned that mentorship is just as valuable for the mentor as it is for the mentee. Listening to fresh perspectives keeps me grounded and reflective. It’s helped me grow as a communicator and leader, reinforcing the importance of empathy, patience, and collaboration.

Technical Outreach

My passion for service also extends into the technical space. I’ve contributed to projects that use AI, computer vision, and embedded systems to solve real problems — from disaster resilience to autonomous response systems. Each of these efforts, at their core, aim to build tools that help people and improve systems that matter.

Service isn’t just about volunteering. It’s about applying your skills and values toward something meaningful. As I continue into advanced research and eventually my professional career, I hope to remain rooted in this mindset.

Core Commitment

Service is one of the Aggie Core Values, and it’s a guiding principle I intend to carry forward. Whether through mentorship, engineering innovation, or simply being someone others can rely on, I aim to leave every space better than I found it — and help others do the same.
