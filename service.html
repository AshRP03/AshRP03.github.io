Service & Initiative

Selfless service is one of the Aggie core values, and I am committed to demonstrating this in both my professional and personal life. As a recipient of a full scholarship through the Craig & Galen Brown Foundation, I have the privilege of giving back to the community by mentoring incoming freshmen who are also beneficiaries of this program. This role has provided me the opportunity to guide students who are just beginning their college journey, helping them adjust to university life, explore academic and extracurricular opportunities, and develop strategies for success. Through this experience, I’ve not only honed my skills as a mentor but have also grown in unexpected ways.

One of the most valuable lessons I’ve learned is that mentorship is a two-way street. While I provide guidance and dvice based on my experiences, I have found that I also learn from those I mentor. These freshmen often bring fresh perspectives and ideas, reminding me that learning is a continuous process that doesn’t depend solely on experience. This exchange of knowledge has shaped my approach to leadership and teamwork, reinforcing my belief in the importance of listening and collaboration.

Outside of my scholarship organization, I am passionate about using my technical skills for the greater good. My studies in computer science and involvement in various research and engineering projects have allowed me to contribute to innovations in areas like automation and AI, particularly in the automotive sector. By combining my technical expertise with a desire to serve others, I hope to create solutions that not only push boundaries but also make a meaningful impact on those around me. This commitment to service will remain a core aspect of my career and life moving forward.
