Service & Initiative

Selfless service is one of the Aggie core values, and I aim to do activities that not just show my technical skills but also my value for giving value to those around me. Through my scholarship org, the Brown Foundation, I mentor incoming freshman who are also beneficiaries of the Craig & Galen Brown Foundation. Through this program, I have not only become a better mentor and teacher, but I have learned more about myself and interestingly, learned how to learn even from those who may be less experienced than me.

In my upcoming final two years of undergraduate school at Texas A&M, I hope to join TANSA, the Texas A&M National Scholar Ambassadors, to expand my ability to welcome and serve upcoming freshman and prospective families to Texas A&M.
