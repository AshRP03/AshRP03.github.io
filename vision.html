The Road Ahead

The next ten years of my ambitions, passions, and exploits.

🚀 Professional Aspirations

My immediate goal is to enter the tech industry after graduation—ideally at a place like Amazon, NVIDIA, or OpenAI—where I can sharpen my skills, contribute meaningfully, and build financial stability. But long-term, I want to work somewhere purpose-driven. Whether it’s engineering intelligent systems at Koenigsegg, contributing to space systems at SpaceX, or researching applied AGI with Valkyrie AI, I want to build systems that inspire, challenge, and push the envelope of what’s possible.

🛠️ Passion Projects & Engineering Hobbies

I’m a lifelong car enthusiast, and I plan to channel that passion into building and modifying my BMW F30. One day, I’d love to own a G87 M2 and transform it into the ultimate driver’s car. Beyond that, I want to become a true hands-on builder—someone who can tune ECUs, wire sensors, fabricate components, and restore classics. I want my garage to reflect who I am as both an engineer and an enthusiast.

🏋️‍♂️ Fitness, Food & Content Creation

Outside the lab and garage, the gym is a cornerstone of my routine. It’s where I challenge myself physically, reset mentally, and stay grounded. Cooking is another creative outlet—I enjoy crafting meals that fuel my training and support my goals. Eventually, I’d love to document my fitness journey and nutritional experiments—whether through videos, blogs, or simple recipes that others can learn from.

📚 Personal Growth & Lifelong Learning

I want to be more than just a software engineer—I want to be a well-rounded thinker, communicator, and global citizen. That means mastering the languages that shape me: Tamil, Hindi, Spanish, and sharpening my command of English. It also means reading more. I’m especially drawn to hard science fiction like The Three-Body Problem, but I also want to become more literate in history, economics, politics, and philosophy.

I’m also reconnecting with hobbies like guitar, learning how to work with my hands, and embracing knowledge that isn’t found in textbooks—whether it’s in the pages of a classic novel or under the hood of a car.

🌍 Travel & Future Exploration

My study abroad experience opened a door I never want to close. I plan to revisit the museums, cities, and roads of Europe—not as a student, but as a builder, creator, and contributor. I want to explore more of Italy, Spain, and Eastern Europe, and experience the culture and landscapes of the Nordics. One day, I’d love to relax in places like Mykonos, Mallorca, or Ibiza. But travel for me isn’t just leisure—it’s part of how I grow, reflect, and find new sources of inspiration.
