Understanding AI and Its Applications

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is reshaping the world, and through projects at Texas A&M and personal research, I’ve had the opportunity to explore cutting-edge AI technologies such as Transformers, Computer Vision, and their applications in real-world projects.

  • Edge-Guided Attention: Collaborated on integrating edge-based methods to guide attention in image segmentation models for enhanced accuracy.

Transformers in AI

  • Transformers in Vision: Leveraged vision transformers (ViTs) for classification and object detection tasks.
  • NLP Transformers: Explored GPT models for language tasks, enhancing understanding of tokenization and self-attention mechanisms.
